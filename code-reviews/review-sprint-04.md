## Architecture Summary

DashForge currently has two bounded product surfaces: a React/Vite/TypeScript
frontend in `frontend/` that renders DashboardSpec-driven mock dashboards
through `DataAdapter`, and a Python generator CLI in `src/dashForge/` that
produces deterministic SQLite databases plus optional SQLite-derived snapshot
exports for healthcare, financial, and saas packs. The main Sprint 4 risk areas
were multi-pack correctness, template-catalog registration integrity, and
preserving the Sprint 2/3 adapter and generation seams without scope drift into
builder, presenter, or live-binding work.
